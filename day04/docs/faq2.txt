ACC Bank - Account Opening Requirements

To open a savings account with ACC Bank, you need the following documents: 
Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, one recent passport-size photograph, and proof of address 
(if different from Aadhaar address). Minimum initial deposit is Rs. 1,000 for a regular 
savings account and Rs. 10,000 for a Premium savings account. Account opening can be done 
online via the ACC Bank app or in person at any branch. Processing time is typically 
1-2 working days.
