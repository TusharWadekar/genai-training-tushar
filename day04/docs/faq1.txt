ACC Bank - Debit Card Policy

To block a lost or stolen debit card, customers can call our 24x7 helpline at 1800-123-4567 
or use the mobile app's "Block Card" feature under Card Services. A replacement card will be 
issued within 5-7 working days and delivered to your registered address. There is no charge 
for blocking a card due to loss or theft, but a replacement card fee of Rs. 200 applies unless 
you are a Premium account holder.
