ACC Bank - UPI Transaction Limits

The daily UPI transaction limit for ACC Bank customers is Rs. 1,00,000 across all UPI apps 
linked to your account. Per-transaction limit is Rs. 25,000 for new UPI registrations within 
the first 24 hours, after which the standard per-transaction limit of Rs. 1,00,000 applies. 
If a UPI transaction fails but the amount is deducted, the amount is automatically refunded 
within 48 hours as per RBI guidelines. For unresolved UPI issues after 48 hours, customers 
should contact UPI support at upi.support@accbank.com.
