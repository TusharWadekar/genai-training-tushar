ACC Bank KYC (Know Your Customer) Requirements

To complete KYC for any ACC Bank account or service, customers must submit: 
Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, one recent passport-size photograph, and address proof 
if different from the Aadhaar-registered address. KYC can be completed online 
via video KYC through the mobile app, or in person at a branch. Video KYC takes 
approximately 10 minutes. KYC must be re-verified every 10 years for low-risk 
accounts and every 2 years for high-risk accounts as per RBI norms.
