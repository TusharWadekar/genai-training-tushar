ACC Bank Debit Card Hotlisting (Blocking) Process

To hotlist (block) a lost or stolen debit card, customers have three options: 
call the 24x7 helpline at 1800-123-4567, use the "Block Card" option in the 
mobile app under Card Services, or visit any branch with valid ID proof. 
Hotlisting takes effect immediately once confirmed. A replacement card is 
issued within 5-7 working days at a fee of Rs. 200, which is waived for 
Premium account holders. There is no charge for the hotlisting action itself.
