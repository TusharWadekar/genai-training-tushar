ACC Bank UPI Transaction Limits and Failures

The daily UPI transaction limit is Rs. 1,00,000 across all linked UPI apps. 
New UPI registrations have a lower per-transaction limit of Rs. 25,000 for 
the first 24 hours. If a UPI transaction fails but the amount is deducted 
from the account, the amount is automatically reversed within 48 hours as 
per RBI guidelines. If the refund does not appear after 48 hours, customers 
should contact upi.support@accbank.com with the transaction reference number.
