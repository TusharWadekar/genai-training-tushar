ACC Bank Net Banking Activation

Net banking can be activated in two ways: through the mobile app under 
Settings > Net Banking > Activate, or by visiting a branch with a filled 
activation form and ID proof. Online activation requires the registered 
mobile number and debit card details for verification. Once activated, 
customers can view statements, transfer funds via NEFT/RTGS/IMPS, and pay 
bills. If net banking is not used for 12 consecutive months, it is 
automatically deactivated for security and must be reactivated manually.
