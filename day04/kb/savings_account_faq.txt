ACC Bank Savings Account FAQ

A regular savings account requires a minimum balance of Rs. 1,000. Premium 
savings accounts require Rs. 10,000 minimum balance but offer higher interest 
rates and free demand drafts. Interest is calculated daily and credited quarterly. 
The standard interest rate on savings accounts is 3.5% per annum. Account holders 
receive a free debit card and mobile banking access. Falling below minimum balance 
incurs a penalty of Rs. 100 per month. Accounts can be closed at any branch with 
a written request; no closure charges apply after 12 months of account opening.
