ACC Bank Charges Schedule

ACC Bank charges are as follows: ATM withdrawal beyond 5 free transactions 
per month costs Rs. 20 per transaction. Cheque book (25 leaves) costs Rs. 100 
after the first free book each year. Duplicate statement requests cost Rs. 50 
per statement. SMS alerts are free for the first year, then Rs. 15 per quarter. 
Demand draft charges are Rs. 50 up to Rs. 10,000 and 0.1% of amount above that, 
capped at Rs. 500.
