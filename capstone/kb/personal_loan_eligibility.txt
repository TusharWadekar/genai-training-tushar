ACC Bank Personal Loan Eligibility

To be eligible for an ACC Bank personal loan, applicants must be salaried 
or self-employed individuals aged 21-60 years with a minimum monthly income 
of Rs. 25,000. A minimum credit score of 700 is required. Loan amounts range 
from Rs. 50,000 to Rs. 15,00,000 with tenures from 12 to 60 months. Interest 
rates range from 10.5% to 18% per annum depending on credit profile. 
Processing fee is 1.5% of the loan amount, subject to a minimum of Rs. 1,000.
