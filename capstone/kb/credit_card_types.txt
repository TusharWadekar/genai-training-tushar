ACC Bank Credit Card Types

ACC Bank offers three credit card variants: Classic, Gold, and Platinum. 
The Classic card has no annual fee and a credit limit up to Rs. 50,000. 
The Gold card has an annual fee of Rs. 500 (waived on spending Rs. 50,000/year) 
and a credit limit up to Rs. 2,00,000. The Platinum card has an annual fee of 
Rs. 2,000 and a credit limit up to Rs. 5,00,000, with airport lounge access and 
travel insurance included. All cards offer 2% cashback on online transactions.
