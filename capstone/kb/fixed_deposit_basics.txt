ACC Bank Fixed Deposit (FD) Basics

ACC Bank Fixed Deposits have a minimum tenure of 7 days and a maximum tenure 
of 10 years. Minimum deposit amount is Rs. 5,000. Interest rates range from 
6.5% (short tenure) to 7.25% (5+ years) per annum. Senior citizens get an 
additional 0.5% interest on all tenures. Premature withdrawal is permitted 
but attracts a penalty of 1% on the applicable interest rate. FDs can be 
opened online via the mobile app or at any branch.
